George Thomas MP

Member of Parliament for Cardiff West (later Speaker of the House of Commons)
Era in the records: 1958–1963

Photograph: Geoff Charles · 10 March 1955 · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Source (as text): https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:George_Thomas_(Lord_Tonypandy)_and_his_mother_(1460789).jpg · period photograph (10 March 1955); cropped to him alone from a photograph with his mother. Shown as published; resized, cropped to the subject.

On the record

Asked about Cardiff planning records in a written question (18 Nov 1958) and took part in the Commons exchanges on Western Ground Rents Ltd in January and February 1963. The 1963 texts do not mention Llandough or the farm.
