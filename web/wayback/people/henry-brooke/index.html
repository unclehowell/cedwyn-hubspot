Henry Brooke MP

Minister of Housing and Local Government and Minister for Welsh Affairs, 1957–1961
Era in the records: 1958

Photograph: Elliot & Fry · June 1950 · Public domain · Source (as text): https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Henry_Brooke_1950.jpg · period-adjacent portrait (June 1950). Shown as published; resized.

On the record

His ministry held the 1958 public local inquiry into objections to the Glamorgan County Council Development Plan (Area No. 2). In a Commons written answer of 18 Nov 1958 he dealt with a question on that inquiry.
